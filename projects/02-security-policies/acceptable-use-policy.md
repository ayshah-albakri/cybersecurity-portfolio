> FalconPay is a fictional company. This document is a portfolio sample.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how FalconPay's information systems, devices, and data may be used, to protect customers, employees, and the company from security incidents and legal risk.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who use FalconPay devices, accounts, networks, or data, whether in the office or remotely.

## 3. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Security Lead | Owns the policy, monitors compliance, and handles exceptions |
| IT Operations | Configures devices and systems to enforce the policy |
| Managers | Make sure their teams read and follow the policy |
| All users | Follow the policy and report incidents |
| HR | Includes the policy in onboarding and handles disciplinary action |

## 4. Policy statements

### 4.1 General use

1. Company systems must be used mainly for business purposes. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect work or security.
2. Users must not use company systems for illegal activity, harassment, or to access inappropriate content.
3. Users must not install unapproved software. Requests go through the IT service desk.
4. Users must not connect unapproved devices (personal USB drives, wireless access points, or network equipment) to company systems or the office network.

### 4.2 Accounts and access

5. Each user must use their own account. Sharing accounts or passwords is not allowed.
6. Users must only access data they need for their job (least privilege).
7. Users must lock their screen when leaving their device, and devices must lock automatically after 5 minutes.

### 4.3 Data handling

8. Customer data and confidential data must only be stored in approved company systems.
9. Users must not send confidential data to personal email or personal cloud storage.
10. Confidential documents must not be left on desks or printers (clean desk rule).

### 4.4 Email and phishing

11. Users must not open unexpected attachments or links, even from known senders, without verifying them.
12. Suspected phishing must be reported using the "Report Phishing" button within 1 hour.
13. Requests to change payment details or share credentials must be verified by phone using a known number.

### 4.5 Mobile devices and remote work

14. Company laptops must have full disk encryption enabled and be enrolled in mobile device management.
15. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 1 hour so they can be remotely wiped.
16. Remote access must use the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi must not be used without the VPN.

### 4.6 Monitoring

17. FalconPay monitors the use of company systems for security purposes, in line with applicable law. Users should have no expectation of privacy when using company systems.

## 5. Training

All users must complete security awareness training during onboarding and every year after, and take part in phishing simulations.

## 6. Compliance and exceptions

- Compliance is checked through device management reports, access reviews, and phishing simulation results.
- Exceptions must be requested in writing, approved by the Security Lead, and reviewed every 6 months.
- Violations may lead to disciplinary action, in line with HR procedures.
